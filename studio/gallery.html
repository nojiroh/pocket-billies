Tags

pb-tag, sized for a battle plate's tag row: the three statuses (glyph and code in their colours); boosts and drops, the arrow first (▲ teal, ▼ red), the stat and its pips (1 to 6); the short effects with their turns left (none: no count); "+N" and a stat back at 0. Every tag says its word to a screen reader.

Chips

pb-chip, on pb-coin's API (kind, value, label; it counts as pb-coin does): the six currencies in their families, then map nodes and messages as label chips (the family's glyph), and the small size.

Blips

pb-proc, when a Perk, held item, trait or type passive fires: its glyph in its family's colour and a short line, on the battle's dark glass (the look only: UI-16b shows them by the plates).

Explainers

pb-info, what a tap on a mark opens (long-press in battle): the mark, its word and one line. A tap on it or Escape closes it. Tap the tag to open one.
