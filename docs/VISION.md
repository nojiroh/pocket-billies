# Vision: Pocket Billies, fresh base (0.1.0 alpha)

The single source of truth for what the game is becoming. Phase files implement it. Items marked (ASK) are not decided; offer options and wait for me.

## Fresh start
- New base. Version numbering restarts at 0.1.0 alpha. The old game is "legacy" (branch + tag + zip), kept only for reference and reuse.
- No save compatibility. Keep the strong parts (renderer, battle engine, Studio, Firebase and friends, debug tools) and rework the rest.

## Setting and story
- Still set in the Hollow Earth, but told as an alternative-future story.
- Opening (scripted, not player-controlled): a map-like scene of a cyberpunk city with tall neon buildings and flying cars. A silhouette walks along the street below. A strange portal opens. When the figure steps through, the game goes into the existing "falling through the air" sequence, then arrives in the Hollow Earth. (Phase 11.)
- A shadowy, mysterious organization works in the background, in the spirit of the FBC from Control and the SCP Foundation (secretive, contains and studies the anomalous). It must be ORIGINAL: its own name, logo, terms and lore, with no copied names, text or terminology from either. (ASK: its name, goals and how often it appears. Phase 15.)
- Possible contrast to confirm (ASK): cyberpunk surface city above, retro-futuristic solarpunk Hollow Earth within.

## How the game plays
- The fitness step tracking is REMOVED completely: real-world steps no longer drive the game (no step costs, no step-driven walking, no hatching by steps). It becomes a classic RPG where you walk the maps yourself with a joystick (as in alpha 0.4.1), the maps blend seamlessly into each other and rooms open in place. (ASK: what replaces each step-based mechanic, phase 08.)
- World map: always 3D, shown as the inside of a half sphere, like a bowl. It shows clouds and simplified terrain, is intuitive on a phone, keeps all existing towns and routes (same connections), and gives everything new, sensible positions that respect how the walkable maps join each other. (Phase 10.)
- Locations and maps are reworked to fit a retro-futuristic solarpunk vibe.
- The player has a small ranch in the starting town that they can customize. (Phase 12.)

## Two kinds of creatures
- Billies (existing): satirical billionaire creatures, tax and market battle mechanics, slimmed roster, swappable parts, breeding and funny "abominations". (Phase 16.)
- Robots (new): inspired by Medabots, similar in role to Billies. The player builds their own from many parts. Each robot has a torso with head, a left arm, a right arm, legs and a gem that acts as its soul. Their battle mechanics differ from Billies and are more like Medabots. Parts can be upgraded and are found, crafted or won in trainer battles. (Phase 17.) Use original names and designs.

## Presentation
- Portraits: only the eyes and mouth animate; the head may move a little. The old portrait system is replaced. 32-bit pixel art. (Phase 07.)
- Art targets: terrain textures and the airship style (low-poly, 32 px/m). Flat props stay flat. (Phase 09.)

## Keeps
Walking maps, seamless joins and rooms that are bigger on the inside than the outside (kept and improved), friends, trading and battling (to be extended to robots and parts, phase 18), the Studio (extended), Firebase accounts, the debug tools and my phone-testing workflow.
