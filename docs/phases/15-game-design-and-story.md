# Phase 15 - Game design and story

## Goal
A written design that makes the satire and the new mechanics the point, and a story bible for the Hollow Earth alternative future and its hidden organization. Do this early; phases 16 and 17 depend on it. Read docs/VISION.md.

## Tasks: design
1. Write a short design doc: the core loop as a classic RPG (walk the connected maps, use the bowl map for overview and airship travel, battle with Billies and robots, manage the ranch, story missions), the progression curve, and what makes each region feel different.
2. Make the tax and market mechanics (taxing, bargaining, hedging, rent, market shock) central to how Billy battles are won, not just flavor. Propose concrete changes and ask me.
3. Make exploring rewarding: secrets, mini-games, shops with personality, not only the next battle.
4. Review and tighten the first 30 minutes: opening, fall, arrival, first dialogue, the ranch, the first battle. The scope (hundreds of items, ~49 locations) is invisible if the opening is not fun.
5. Legal and tone: fictionalize creatures that parody real named people (for example Ballmerilla, Gabenwalrus, Tiktokferret) into satire of types rather than individuals; keep the structure and slogan away from Pokemon's, and robot designs and terms away from Medabots'.

## Tasks: story and organization
6. Story bible: the Hollow Earth as an alternative future, how the portal and the fall connect the cyberpunk surface to the interior, what the player is looking for, and how the gems and robots tie in (propose; ask me).
7. The shadowy organization: secretive, contains and studies the anomalous, in the spirit of the FBC from Control and the SCP Foundation. It must be fully original: a new name, logo, terminology and lore, with no copied names, text or classification schemes. Propose 3 name options, its goals, how it looks and feels (agents, redacted documents, hidden facilities, how it relates to the ranch, Billies and robots), and how often it appears. Ask me before locking any of it.
8. A mystery pacing plan: which clues appear where, from the opening hint (phase 11) to the first reveals.

## Ask me before
Every mechanic change; the organization's name, goals and prominence; renaming any creature, region or slogan; the cyberpunk-surface / solarpunk-interior contrast.

## Done when
Design doc and story bible written and agreed, first-30-minutes changes listed, parodies of real people replaced, tag `phase-15-done`.
