The script

What the writer types. Brackets are the tags; the line playing now is marked.

    Try a line

    Tags

    [happy] [sad] [angry] [surprised] [smug]The face, at the start of a line (as today).
    [shout] [whisper] [think]The bubble's shape and voice, right after the face.
    [wait]A "…" bubble first, for a beat before the line.
    [b]…[/b] [i]…[/i]Bold with a pop; a slant for the stressed word.
    [big] [small]Size. Big uses the pixel voice font.
    [c=gold] red teal greyColour, always with weight too.
    [wave] [shake]Letters bob; letters jitter (once).
    [slow] [fast]Typing at half or double speed.
    [p] [p=0.8]A pause, 0.4 s or the seconds given.
    [face=angry]Changes the face mid-line.
    [emote=sweat]! ? sweat anger sparkle note heart.
    [stamp=denied]The Bureau's stamp: denied, approved, pending, filed, void.
    [cut] or a final —The line breaks off; the next one interrupts it.

    What to look for

    The stack: the last two lines stay above the new one, smaller and dimmer; speakers swap sides; one speaker's lines in a row link up and share a portrait.

    Skins: the Bureau's ruled form with a stamp, Chosen's gold with one shine, Kappi's leaf and their "…", Blake's pop-art slant, your neon.

    Motion: bubbles spring in from the speaker's side with a small tilt that the text doesn't follow; only the newest bubble breathes. Calm and Reduced turn the extras off.

    Faces: random blinks, a mouth that opens on vowels, the head nudged a pixel on a spring for a nod, a jolt or a shake, emotes, and the circle bouncing.
